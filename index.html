01 — Colour

Cream is the ground. Navy is the anchor.

Six colours carry every piece Lev has produced. Cream — not white — is the page. Royal carries the voice, sky does the moving, navy closes, and black belongs to the wordmark alone. A campaign does not get to introduce a seventh. Values below are read from the master vector file, not sampled off compressed artwork.

03 — Logo

One name. Nine lockups. No tenth.

Always Lev Family Health. When a location needs naming: Lev Family Health — Monsey, Lev Family Health — Pomona. No location and no service line gets its own logo. Nine lockups cover every ground the brand meets; if a situation seems to need a tenth, it needs one of these nine at a different size. All artwork below is vector, extracted from the master Lev logos.ai.

Lev Family Health

Lev Women's Health

The sub-brand keeps the wordmark and swaps the mark colourway. It also carries an alternate figure mark — head, shoulders, heart — that no other service line may use.

Marks alone

The mark drops the wordmark only where the name is already present or the space is too small to set it: avatars, favicons, seals, watermarks.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space equals the height of the chevron

Measured on all four sides. Nothing enters that zone — not type, not a photo edge, not a shape accent.

Minimum width

1.25 in in print, 140 px on screen. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do — pick the lockup built for the ground you are standing on. There is one for white, black, navy and magenta.
Don't — recolour a lockup by hand, rotate the mark, flatten it to one tone outside the mono files, or build a logo for a location.

04 — Shape accents & page architecture

Three shapes, lifted out of the mark.

The quarter arc, the square and the chevron anchor photography and break the grid. Two per page, maximum, on opposing corners, bleeding off the edge. Half a square reads as confidence; a centred square reads as clip art.

Quarter arc · sky · in front of a photo's bottom-left corner

Square · royal · behind a photo's top-right corner

Chevron · royal or sky · a bullet or a pointer, never large

Photo frame

Photography is warm, clinical and real — a provider and a patient mid-interaction, natural light. Crop tight on the interaction: hands, eye contact, a stethoscope on a child's chest.

07 — The rules we hold to

What the design system can't fix.

Carried over from the brand guidelines deck. These bind copy as tightly as the tokens bind colour.

One name

Always Lev Family Health. Locations are modifiers: Lev Family Health — Monsey. Lev Family Health — Pomona.

One look

One logo, one colour system, one voice. No separate identity for any location or service line.

One message, in this order

One practice for the whole family. Two locations, same practice, same record. Walk-in availability when you need it. Insurance stability and access.

What we lead with

Primary care first. Walk-in availability is a convenience for our patients, not the headline.

Our promise

We will always do our best, work with heart, and treat you like family.

What we don't say

No wait-time promises. No superlatives. No clinical claims.

08 — Open item

One inconsistency in circulation.

Fonts are licensed to JAM and the master vector file is in hand — both previously open items are closed. One thing the master file surfaced is worth a decision.

A "Lev Health" descender is running in produced work, and it is not in the master file. The logo suite contains two descenders: Family Health and Women's Health. Several ads in the Ready Ads folder close with a shortened Lev Health lockup, and at least one closes with the wordmark alone. Either the short lockup gets added to the master file as a sanctioned tenth, or the ads get corrected to Lev Family Health. It should not stay in both states.
