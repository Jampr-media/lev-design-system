Foundations — Logo

One name: Lev Family Health. Locations are modifiers, never separate brands — "Lev Family Health — Monsey", "Lev Family Health — Pomona". Nine lockups exist and they cover every case; if a situation seems to need a tenth, it needs one of these nine at a different size. All files here are vector, extracted from the master Lev logos.ai.

Lev Women's Health

The sub-brand keeps the wordmark and swaps the mark colourway. It also has an alternate figure mark — head, shoulders, heart — that no other service line may use.

Marks alone

The mark carries the brand without the wordmark only where the name is already present or the space is too small to set it: avatars, favicons, seals, watermarks.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space equals the height of the chevron

Measured on all four sides. Nothing — type, photo edge, shape accent — enters that zone.

Minimum width

1.25 in in print. 140 px on screen. Below that, use the mark alone.

Do — pick the lockup built for the ground you are on. There is one for white, black, navy and magenta.
Don't — recolour a lockup by hand, rotate the mark, flatten it to one tone outside the mono files, or build a logo for a location.
