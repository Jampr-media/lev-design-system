Foundations — Colour

Six colours carry every piece Lev has produced. Cream is the ground, navy is the anchor, royal carries the voice, sky does the moving, and black belongs to the wordmark alone. Values read from the master vector file.

Cream
#F9F6DF
--lev-cream
Primary light ground. The signature. Plain white is never the page background.
Navy
#091F3E
--lev-navy
Closing band, alert grounds, and all body text on light.
Royal
#2A50A3
--lev-royal
Logo chevron, display headlines on cream, primary chips.
Sky
#55B7E7
--lev-sky
Logo arcs, shape accents, secondary chips, eyebrows on navy.
Black
#000000
--lev-black
The wordmark, and only the wordmark. Body text is navy.
White
#FFFFFF
--lev-white
Cards floating on cream. Never a full-page ground.
