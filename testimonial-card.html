Components — Testimonial card

White card, generous padding, justified body, initials and a relative timestamp. Patients are identified by initials only. Reviews run verbatim — no tidying, no composite quotes.

My baby was never so calm before. The doctor was amazing, the place was beautiful, and the support made everything so much easier

I felt like someone was really listening. They helped me get the right care for my child, and the whole visit felt calm, warm, and easy

Do — stagger the cards. The offset is what makes it feel like a conversation rather than a list.
Don't — write a review, edit one for grammar, or attribute a quote to a full name.
